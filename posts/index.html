Evidence standard

Fact, interpretation, and opinion stay separate.

Primary or closest-to-source evidence is preferred for factual claims. Sentiment and opinion are useful when the question is what people believe, but they are not treated as proof that the belief itself is true.

Why publish the process?

The goal is not only to show finished conclusions. This journal makes the analytical process visible: source discovery, cleaning, competing hypotheses, corrections, negative findings, and the reasoning that connects data to a defensible conclusion.
