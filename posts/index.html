Evidence standard

Claims should match the evidence.

Measured results, interpretation, and opinion stay separate. I prefer reproducible methods, documented limitations, and primary or closest-to-source evidence when making factual claims.

Engineering standard

A useful result should also be trustworthy.

Data quality, validation, explicit assumptions, visible failures, testing, recovery, and accessible presentation are part of the work, not cleanup after the work.
