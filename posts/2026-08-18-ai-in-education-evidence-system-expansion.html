Research Milestone • August 18, 2026

AI in Education expanded from a sentiment project into a multi-layer evidence system.

The project now combines large-scale student survey analysis with U.S. educator evidence, Census business AI adoption, O*NET task structure, OpenAI occupation exposure, and five years of BLS employment and wage data.

Open Full Case StudyView Repository
What changed

The question became bigger than opinion.

The current workflow separates perception from measurable outcomes. Student and educator attitudes are analyzed as one evidence layer. Business adoption, occupational exposure, employment, wages, layoffs, company attribution, and long-run educational outcomes are treated as separate layers that can agree or contradict each other.

Current scale

23,218 student records and 174 variables.

Census BTOS business data across geography, industry, and business size.

O*NET plus OpenAI occupation and task exposure data.

BLS OEWS employment and wage data for 2021 through 2025.

Preliminary Pattern

Use is rising while concern remains substantial.

71.4% adoption

Among respondents answering the student-survey adoption question, 71.4% reported having used ChatGPT.

63.4% access benefit

Students were especially positive about improved access to knowledge and study efficiency.

36.0% critical thinking

Students were much less convinced that ChatGPT improves critical-thinking skills.

Risks remain visible

Cheating, plagiarism, misinformation, and learning displacement all received substantial agreement.

Next analysis

AI exposure × actual employment and wages

The next joined analysis will connect OpenAI occupation exposure and O*NET task data to BLS OEWS 2021-2025 to test whether highly exposed occupations actually performed worse, were augmented, experienced hiring suppression, or show little detectable effect yet.

Research discipline

Exposure is not treated as replacement, correlation is not treated as causation, and layoffs will not be labeled AI-caused without stronger attribution evidence.
